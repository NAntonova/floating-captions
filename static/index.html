💡 FaceTime Audio Capturing Guide

  1. Select Mixed or System Audio capture mode.
  2. Click Listen and Chrome will prompt you to share a screen or window.
  3. Select the FaceTime window or your Entire Screen.
  4. CRITICAL: Check the "Share system audio" checkbox in the Chrome share popup window! Otherwise FaceTime speaker audio won't be captured.
  5. Safari/Firefox Fallback: Create an Aggregate Device with BlackHole and map it as your default Mic source.
